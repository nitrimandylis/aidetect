In conclusion, the comparative analysis demonstrates that RFID technology offers distinct yet complementary benefits when applied to monitoring contexts within educational and healthcare environments. While RFID systems in schools enable streamlined attendance tracking, resource allocation, and enhanced safety through real‑time location visibility, hospitals leverage the same capabilities for patient flow optimization, asset management, and infection‑control monitoring, thereby reducing operational inefficiencies and improving outcomes. The juxtaposition reveals that the primary advantages—automation, traceability, and data‑driven decision‑making—are universally applicable, suggesting that the merits of RFID implementation transcend sectoral boundaries. However, the extent to which benefits are realized is contingent upon tailored integration strategies, robust governance, and ongoing evaluation of ethical and privacy considerations.
