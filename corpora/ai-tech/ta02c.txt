In conclusion, this investigation reveals a statistically significant positive correlation between the density of Pandanus heliacorpus individuals and the abundance of Macrobrachium spp. within the selected freshwater habitats, suggesting that structural complexity provided by this keystone plant directly influences crustacean population dynamics. The findings support the hypothesis that habitat heterogeneity drives distribution patterns, reinforcing the ecological importance of preserving dense P. heliacorpus stands for sustaining local fishery resources. Limitations related to seasonal sampling bias and the exclusion of abiotic variables are acknowledged, and recommendations for future multi‑year, broader‑scale studies are outlined to strengthen causal inference. Overall, the study underscores the interdependence of plant community structure and invertebrate fisheries management in tropical island ecosystems.
