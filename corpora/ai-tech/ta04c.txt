In conclusion, this investigation demonstrated that reflux time exerts a measurable but secondary influence on the synthesis of benzocaine, with the optimal duration identified as 60 minutes for balancing yield and purity. Extended reflux beyond this point led to diminishing returns, characterized by marginal yield increases that were offset by the formation of impurities, likely from ester hydrolysis or side reactions. While a purity of approximately 85% was achievable after recrystallization, the persistent presence of unreacted aminobenzoic acid highlighted the inherent limitations of Fischer esterification for this substrate; consequently, alternative methods such as acid chloride coupling should be considered for applications demanding high purity. Thus, the reflux parameter is effective for controlling reaction completeness but requires strict regulation to avoid product degradation.
