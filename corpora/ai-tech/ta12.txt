This section presents the experimental results graphically and analyses the relationships they reveal. The initial data establish the relationship between observed rotation and the concentration of glucose in solution, with the raw measurements tabulated below and the complete data set provided in the appendix. Solutions of glucose and fructose were subsequently combined in varying proportions in order to determine whether the resulting rotation could be predicted from the concentrations of the constituent monosaccharides. The results of this final stage are presented in the tables that follow and represented graphically thereafter.
