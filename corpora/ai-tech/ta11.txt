Wireless in Local Loop technology has been proposed as a means of delivering both voice and data services to rural India without the prohibitive expense of laying physical cable. The system transmits telephone signals by radio from a local exchange to receivers within a radius of approximately ten kilometres, providing reliable voice communication alongside internet access at usable speeds. When supported by solar-powered relay stations, this radius can be extended considerably, making the technology particularly suitable for regions that lack a dependable electricity supply. It therefore represents a pragmatic response to the infrastructural constraints of rural deployment.
