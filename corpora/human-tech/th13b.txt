Secondly, we noticed that during slipping, the linear velocity increased quite significantly. Again since the velocity measurements were quite precise (as verified by one of the calibration test performed), we are fairly certain that the difference between the experimental and theoretical data in graph 3 is not due to experimental errors, whether it be systematic or random, but in fact due to slipping.
