Extending the line OA and OB, we obtain the chords OA' and OB'. Consider the angles that these legs form with the radius OC, angles a and B - if ball A is to rebound and strike ball B then a must equal B (angle of incidence = angle of reflection). Furthermore, if we extend lines OA, OC, and OB to the circle at A' and B' then we form 3 new angles. The angle between OA' and the x-axis is noted 4, the angle between OC and the x-axis is u, and the angle between OB' and the x-axis is a.
