During puberty (group II) the rod efficiency for males rises, whereas it drops to an overall minimum for females. During the puberty the rod efficiency of the males seems to be 1% better than that of females. After puberty (group III) the values are close to the same and males reach their peak in rod efficiency. Group IV and V show that female rod efficiency is again better than male rod efficiency.
