Possible solutions can be found at points 1, 2, 3 and 4 as shown on the diagram. In this case we have four places where we can strike one of the balls so that it rebounds and hits the other. However that being said, if ball A was aimed at point 3, then ball A would "go through" ball B and then bounce off the border to hit the ball back. In other words for ball A, mathematically points 1, 2, 3 and 4 are all solutions, but realistically only points 1, 2, and 4 are solutions because ball B would block the path of ball A before it can reach point 3.
