Immediately, one is compelled to ask: "How much time does this process take" In other words, how many times must the function be iterated for a sequence to reach 1? How does the length of a trajectory vary for different flight numbers? These questions lead to the introduction of the concept of "flight time," which corresponds to the number of altitudes that the forward orbit of a given flight comprises before it reaches 1. Hence the "total stopping time function," denoted by on), can be defined as the function that assigns to every natural number n its flight time.
