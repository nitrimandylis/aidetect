The mutarotation of glucose possibly was occurring while doing the experiment. Since the results were recorded on a series of days, the observed rotation of glucose or fructose may have varied. The collection of data would ideally have been conducted on the same day, so as to minimise the possibility of mutarotation of glucose. If this is not possible, it would be alternatively ideal to make up the monosaccharide solutions and use them immediately rather than leave them in solution for the duration of the experiment.
