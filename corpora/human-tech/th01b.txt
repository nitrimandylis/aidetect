As Siberian hamsters are nocturnal animals, in the normal day/night cycle their locomotor activity peaked during the night and generally fell during the day. In all three hamsters the highest level of activity occurred approximately from 00:00 to 03:00. When the hamsters were placed in constant darkness in the first part of the experiment, this pattern of activity and inactivity continued to repeat itself, which agrees with what was mentioned in the hypothesis: their endogenous (internal) clocks continued to operate in the constant environment, even without an external stimulus or cue. However, when light pulses were given during subjective night and the hamsters were given time to adjust in the second part of the experiment, this pattern of locomotor activity shifted (was delayed) by approximately three hours, as shown on the graph on the previous page.
