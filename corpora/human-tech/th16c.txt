The power of reason, already apparently the (semiofficial) dominant player of the ways of knowing, is likely to be bolstered via the use of automata to model various objects - it serves to further declare that reason can not only understand, but also simulate and recreate all. In other words, the advancement of cellular automata as models may serve to heighten the idea that reason generates tangible results greater than that of any of the other ways of knowing, an opinion already elevated by modern science and philosophy. Therefore, cellular automata's influence will be similar to that of what was once called natural philosophy.
