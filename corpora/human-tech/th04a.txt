TLC plates consist of a backing material (e.g. plastic) and are coated with an adsorbent (e.g. silica gel). Disposable plates also contain an inorganic fluorescent agent enabling the spots on the chromatogram to show up as blue marks under a low intensity UV lamp. The mixture being tested is dissolved in a solvent and the different compounds (seen as spots under the UV lamp) move different distances up the plate due to their differing affinity for the stationary and mobile phases. Spotters (used to place the solution onto the baseline) can be made by drawing out capillary tubes under a hot flame and breaking them at the restriction.
