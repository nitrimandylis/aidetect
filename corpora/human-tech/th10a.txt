Alhazen made reference to this problem in one of his published works entitled Optics and presented it in the form, "Find the given point on a spherical mirror at which a ray of light coming from a given point must strike in order to be reflected". Nowadays, this problem is often referred to as the "Billiard Problem" because it involves locating the point on the edge of a circular billiard table at which a cue ball at a given point must be aimed in order to carom (bounce) once off the edge and strike another ball at a second given point.
