Organismal senescence in humans starts roughly around the age of 30. Since this study was conducted world wide, whereas the people I conducted the experiment on are all from countries with high standards of healthcare (Singapore and Germany, the figure should be higher. Therefore the decline in rod efficiency starting around the age of 40 is due to organismal senescence. Having established this, the only remaining question is why cone efficiency starts decreasing immediately from group I until group V? This could be an area of further study.
