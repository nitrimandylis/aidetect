The components of the puzzle board are either cells that already have values (instance of JLabel [Orac]) or cells that can be edited by the user (instances of JTextField [Oraf]). The contents of the board are stored in the database as strings and a loop through this String is used for displaying the board in the GUI (Figure 12).
