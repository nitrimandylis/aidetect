Linked with the notion of belatedness of paintings and limitations of representations, Tuymans paints deliberately in a weak style that breaks completely with his virtuosity. We see 'painterly clumsiness 910: awkwardness by peculiar use of short brushstrokes (see Der diagnostische Blick IV), drawings which often seem rigid, uncertain or slightly distorted. As Tuymans paints on flat canvas, his modeling has no depth. The compositions are simple, more has been deleted than added (empty rooms), often restricted to the presentation of one frontal object. The paintings themselves are executed in no more than one day.
