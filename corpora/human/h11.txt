Because the two deities were so different, the Apollonian Greeks rejected Dionysus and his rather violent power. However in the book Nietzsche points out the dependency that lies in the relationship between Apollo and Dionysus. The barbaric aspects of Dionysus were necessary for the Apollonian Greeks as they were the reality and thus the truth, a foundation on which the Apollonian created the semblance of beauty. Meanwhile Dionysus needed the existence of Apollo for redemption. Thus the two worked as opposing forces struggling with one another and yet at the same time emerged as an intertwining whole.
