The subculture in favor of private health insurance consists primarily of the insurers themselves. In the other subculture stand the government and lobbyists who advocate for programs of public health (such as universal health insurance). Between the two, however, remain numerous special-interest groups, ranging from doctors to pharmaceutical companies to employers. These latter parties seek to fulfill their financial needs, and since private insurance succeeds in doing so, they typically side with that subculture. However, because they are not the principle players of either subculture, their influence on the health care question are only included as necessary.
