While in Vietnam, Trevino was only able to do a few paintings. One painting of a Vietnamese woman carrying her baby on her back is displayed at the Smithsonian. His canvas for this painting was a brown paper bag that he painted with tempera. However, he did not get to finish the painting because on the third day of a search-and destroy mission, his platoon was trapped by sniper fire. As Trevino tried to back away from the sniper fire, he was hit in the leg and fell against a booby trap, which sent shrapnel through his arm and body.
