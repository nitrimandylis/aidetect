A third informal opportunity to learn Spanish is from family members. 16.3% of highschoolers interviewed reported speaking Spanish the most with family. Of those, 68% mentioned brothers. sisters, or relatives. This breakdown alludes to two points: first, that the younger generation is more conversant in Spanish than their parents and, second, that family working outside of the house is more conversant in Spanish than those remaining in the rural home. Men leave Domingo Savio for military training and required term of service, and also to seek urban work in factories or with the police (rarely). Women who leave rural homes nearly always work as maids in Asuncion, Buenos Aires, or even Spain. Housecleaning appears easier to encounter than the jobs men seek and also more consistent, lasting until women decide to return and establish their own homes back in the campo.
