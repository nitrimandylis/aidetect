As has been revealed, both groups had a positive attitude to the development of this sport, seeing in it positive repercussions for whoever supported it, as they would appear to be supporting the people themselves. The State's benevolent view of the game, with politicians trying to become associated with it, does indeed reflect the way in which the State was attempting to forge a healthy, 'civilised' people among the citizenry in general. In the same way, the Church's attempts to integrate football into the religious lifestyle expected of its followers, are a microcosm of the Church's concern with losing followers and subsequent compromise on some of the facets of life that they were not previously willing to give way on.
