The DSM-IV characterizes autism as a disorder in which behavioral, social, and linguistic delays existed comprehensively. The most renowned feature in association with autism is mental retardation (APA, 2000). By definition, the onset of autism must be prior to 3 years of age, however it is difficult to identify under 2 years old (APA, 2000). Since most signs of autism are present during infancy and will develop with growth, early detection of the disorder can occur, thus allowing treatment to begin.
