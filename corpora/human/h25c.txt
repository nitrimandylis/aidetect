Elvira Cisneros is a prominent community service leader and the wife of Henry Cisneros, the first Hispanic mayor of a major American city and secretary of the U.S Department of Housing and Urban Development under President Bill Clinton's cabinet. She is one of the many Westside citizens that donated cuttings of roses to place in front of the Virgen de Guadalupe Veladora as part of a project organized by the Guadalupe Cultural Arts Center 63 It is another cultural tradition evoked by Trevino's work that provides visual identity for the center with enhancement.
