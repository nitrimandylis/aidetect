Theophilus of Syracuse, another character who supposedly made a pact with the devil, comes from a story in the sixth-century A.D. The legend of St. Cyprian deals again with magical feats conducted with the aid of a demon and is dealt with primarily in the 17* century play El Magico Prodigioso by Calderon. Source: Brockbank, pp. 12-13. There were a series of possible original "Fausts' throughout the 16" century, especially in Germanic areas. Source: Brockbank, p. 12.
