However, the V-Weapons brought their share of troubles to the Germans as well as to the Allies. The project itself had over 12,000 working at Peenumünde at its height, as well as thousands more working as forced slave labour to produce the rockets in the underground production facilities at Mittelwerk, Wiener Neustadt and Friedrichshafe. ' This extraordinary example of manpower was comparable to that of the Manhattan project in size and scope. Money as well as people, was piled into the project.
