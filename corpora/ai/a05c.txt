In conclusion, *The Lord of the Rings* presents a nuanced vision of feminine empowerment that transcends the outwardly patriarchal framework of Middle-earth. While the political and martial spheres are undeniably male-dominated, Tolkien invests his central female figures—Galadriel, Éowyn, and Arwen—with a profound agency rooted in wisdom, resilience, and moral choice. Their power is not one of conquest but of preservation, healing, and the defiance of despair. Ultimately, the text suggests that true strength lies in these quieter, transformative acts, redefining heroism to include endurance and the courageous reshaping of fate, thereby weaving the feminine principle into the very fabric of Middle-earth’s salvation.
