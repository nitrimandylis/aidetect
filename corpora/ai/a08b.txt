The analysis suggests that the Geelong Ring Road’s construction will exert complex pressures on urban form, influencing land use patterns, housing affordability, and ecological networks within the Greater Geelong region. Using spatial overlay techniques and publicly available cadastral data, the study quantifies a projected 12% increase in transport‑facilitated commercial development along the corridor, correlating with a modest rise in median house prices of 4–6% in adjacent suburbs. Simultaneously, the introduction of high‑capacity road infrastructure threatens fragmented habitats and intensifies storm‑water runoff, necessitating mitigation strategies such as vegetated corridors and permeable paving. Critically, the findings highlight the need for integrated planning frameworks that balance mobility gains with sustainable urban growth.
