Although indigenous Australians live further away from educational services than non-indigenous Australians, distance decay can only play a partial role in poor indigenous retention rates. The effects of colonisation such as racism and the disregard to indigenous culture, coupled with their current social disadvantages including low personal wealth and lack of indigenous teachers, must also be taken into account. In all, the reasons behind the low retention rates of indigenous students are complex involving both geographical and social factors, rooted in Australia's colonial history.
