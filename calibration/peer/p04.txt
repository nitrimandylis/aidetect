My client is Alex Papadopoulos, who attends an IB school like I do. Alex wants to get good grades and be responsible, however he is struggling to keep up with his tasks for school leading to missing deadlines and lots of stress. That is mainly because he can't remember all of his assignments and deadlines. In the IB it can be very challenging to manage your time effectively and stay on track with the countless of responsibilities you have (like Internal Assessments). He has tried to buy a calendar and write his tasks on there, but this method was inconvenient since he cannot access it when he is not at home. To fix that problem he also tried keeping notes of his tasks and responsibilities on his phone, but this was not efficient either, because manually typing each task was very time-consuming and the lack of an organized system made it difficult to track deadlines effectively.
